# Log mill

A single page that plans boards cut from one elliptical log. Enter the diameter across, the diameter vertical, a common saw kerf shown as a fraction (default 1/8) or a custom inch value, and for each board either a fixed count or “as many as will fit.” Desired sizes are placed in the order entered; leftover wood is filled with other common sizes. Each cut is one straight kerf across the current piece only (the log, then a slab or a cant), splitting that piece in two. A finished board keeps its thickness and width; later cuts only separate leftover wood along its side. Clear resets the form. The layout is a practical search, not a proven optimum.

Every cut takes a kerf, including the outer faces, and that kerf band has to lie inside the log.
